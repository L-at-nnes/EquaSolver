# Product

## Register

product

## Users

General public. Anyone who needs to solve an equation or run a quick calculation: students, parents helping with homework, curious adults. Used on phone or laptop, in short sessions, in a bright room as often as not.

## Product Purpose

EquaSolver is a calculator and equation solver with 30+ tools (polynomial equations, systems, matrices, calculus, converters, graphs). Success means the answer is on screen in a few seconds with the working shown, in six languages, offline.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. A good scientific instrument: neutral surface, one accent, numbers that are easy to read.

## Anti-references

Neon cyberpunk dashboards, glowing borders, gradient text, particle backgrounds, display fonts in UI labels, card hover theatrics.

## Design Principles

1. The tool disappears into the task: inputs, one primary action, result.
2. One accent, used for actions and selection only.
3. Numbers are the content: monospace, tabular, aligned.
4. Motion only conveys state, 150 to 200 ms.
5. Light first, dark as an equal citizen; both meet contrast requirements.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, visible focus rings, reduced-motion respected, touch targets of at least 40 px.
